# DSA Panel for Mac — Because My Problem Notes Were Everywhere

Anyone prepping for coding interviews knows the mess, problems solved on
LeetCode, notes in a random Notion page, a timer running on your phone
separately, and a spreadsheet you update maybe once a week if you remember.
Nothing talks to anything else, and you lose track of what you've actually
covered.

I built this for myself first, a native Mac app that keeps everything in
one place, what you've solved, which topics still need work, a timer for
practicing under real interview conditions, and space to actually sketch out
your thinking instead of just typing code.

---

## What it does

- **Tracks every problem you've solved**, across whichever platforms you
  practice on, in one list instead of scattered everywhere.
- **Organizes by topic**, so you can see at a glance if you've been avoiding
  graphs or DP.
- **Built-in timer** for practicing under actual interview pressure, not
  untimed and relaxed.
- **A notes and sketch space** next to every problem, for dry-running logic
  the way you'd actually do it on a whiteboard.
- **Everything stays on your machine.** No account, no server, your prep
  history is yours.
